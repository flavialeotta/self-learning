Welcome

Collection of self-learning materials for Bioinformatics and Scientific Software development

This repository is dedicated to learning new skills through publicly available material. It is not only intended for me to organize my own self-learning but also to provide a collection of free, publicly available material online that can be useful to other early-career Bioinformatics Workflow scientists.

Programming languages

  • Rust | Book online and materials by Steve Klabnik, Carol Nichols, and Chris Krycho, with contributions from the Rust Community. Completed modules:
    • Chapter 1: Rust essentials and Cargo (rustup, rustftm, cargo);
    • Chapter 3 (on going): Common Programming Concepts (variables, constants and constant evaluation).
  • C++ | Course online by Codecademy. Completed modules:
    • Variables;
    • Conditionals & Logic;
    • Loops;
    • Vectors;
    • Functions (on going).

Collaborative programming

  • Version control with Git | Materials by SIB (Swiss Institute of Bioinformatics) funded by the Swiss State Secretariat for Education Research and Innovation (SERI) and the European Union’s Horizon Europe Framework Program (grant number 101080997). Completed modules:
    • Day 1: first steps with Git: fully completed. Topics: create and configure a Git repository, make commits, branching, remote collaboration, pull requests (GitHub);

Courses

  • Deep Learning for Life Sciences | Course that is part of the Blended Intensive Program (BIP) action within the 4EU+ alliance of European universities. Materials and lectures recordings are available publicly online. Lecturers: Bartek Wilczynski and Joanna Sulkowska (University of Warsaw), Britta Velten (Heidelberg University), Carl Herrmann and Karl Rohr (Heidelberg University), David Hoksza (Charles University Prag), Dario Malchiodi and Marco Frasca (University of Milano), Elodie Laine and Grégoire Sergeant-Perthuis (Sorbonne University), Martin Schätz (Charles University Prag). Completed modules:
    • Intro and Mathematical foundation to DL.
  • From 06.10.2026: Big Data for Agri-Food: Principles and Tools | Online course provided by Wageningen University e-learning platform, partially supported by the European Union Horizon 2020 Research and Innovation program (Grant #810 775, “Dragon”). Lecturers: Ioannis N. Athanasiadis, Sjoukje Osinga, Christos Pylianidis (Wageningen University & Research).
